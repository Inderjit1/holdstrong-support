HoldStrong Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026.

Your workout progress stays on your device. HoldStrong has no accounts and does not upload your workouts, completion history or effort ratings.

Anonymous analytics. We use TelemetryDeck to count anonymous app events such as onboarding completed, workout completed and paywall viewed. These events are not linked to your identity, contain no personal or health information, and are not used for tracking across other apps or sites.

Purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device.
